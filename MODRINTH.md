# Create: Drugs

A **Create** addon adding fictional drugs, alcohol and nicotine, with Create-based production, side effects, dependence and villager trading.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.264, Create 6.0.10. Needed on both client and server. [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) is optional but needed for viewing recipes.

## Features

- **Crops:** cannabis, coca and tobacco. Craft seeds from wheat seeds plus a vine, cocoa beans or a brown mushroom. Bone meal works and Create harvesters are supported.
- **Create recipes:** fans, millstones, mixers, presses, deployers, spouts and mechanical crafters make joints, cocaine, methamphetamine, LSD, cigars, vapes, nicotine patches and beer. Beer is a real fluid.
- **Effects:** every item gives a short buff with a drawback. Heavy use stacks into overdose penalties.
- **Dependence:** beer and nicotine are addictive. Withdrawal grows worse over time and saves through death and restarts.
- **Vapes:** heavy vaping causes popcorn lung. Patches stop nicotine withdrawal.
- **Villagers:** the Herbalist and Dealer professions trade these items for emeralds.
- **Commands:** `/drugstatus` shows your dependence. `/drugreset <players>` is operator-only.
- **Config:** everything is tunable in `create_drugs-server.toml`, including turning lethal withdrawal off.

All items are in the **Create: Drugs** creative tab. Search `@create_drugs` in JEI to see every recipe.

*Fictional gameplay mechanics only, not simulations of real substances.*
